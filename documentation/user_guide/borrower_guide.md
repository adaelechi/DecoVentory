# Borrower Guide

This guide is for users who wish to browse the decoration catalog or request materials for their own events.

## 🖼️ Browsing Past Work
Navigate to the **Decorations** section to see a gallery of past events handled by our unit. This can give you inspiration for your own event.

## 🛍️ Requesting Materials
1. Go to the **Rent Materials** page.
2. Browse the available catalog of fabrics, props, and lighting.
3. Add the items you need to your request list.
4. Fill in your name, event location, and date.
5. Submit the request.

## 📬 What Happens Next?
- Once submitted, your request will be reviewed by an administrator.
- You will be contacted via the information provided to finalize the arrangement and discuss pickup/delivery.
- Please ensure all materials are handled with care and returned on the agreed date to avoid replacement fees.
